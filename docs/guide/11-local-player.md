# Settlement and local maps: player

Audience: player. Required: the GM has opened a linked local scene.


## Read local places

1. Press Ctrl+F or click Search.
2. Choose a local place.
3. Hover its marker for the preview or double-click for the full card.

Place information follows the GM's discovery and visibility settings.

## Walking and coach trips

The GM selects the destination, passengers and method. A coach trip may include walking to and from a carriage route.

If your character is left behind, the GM's departure creates a separate party for those staying. Ask the GM to merge the groups when they reunite.

The GM controls scene entry, return navigation and the settlement trip planner.
