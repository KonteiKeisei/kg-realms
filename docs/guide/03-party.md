# Prepare the party and map

Audience: GM. Required: [an open realm map](02-realm.md).

Recommended: [Calendaria](01-install.md#first-load-module-check) for travel, watches and camp timekeeping. Set the calendar, date and time before starting a journey.

## Create and designate the party

1. Create a D&D 5e group actor in the Actors sidebar.
2. Add the travelling characters and NPCs to its members.
3. Give each player ownership of their character actor through Foundry's ownership settings.
4. Open Configure Settings, then KG Realms.
5. Drop the group actor into Party token actor.
6. Save the settings.
7. Drag that group actor onto the realm map at the starting location.


Individual character tokens do not replace the group token. Walking speed and camp members come from the group's members.

## Scene override

Use an override if this scene should use a different group from the module-wide selection.

1. Open Configure Scene for the realm.
2. On the Basics tab, find KG Realms party token.
3. Drop a group actor into Scene override, or select its placed token and click Use selected token.
4. Save the scene.

Clear the override to return to the global designation and automatic fallback.

## Prepare destinations on a custom realm

Skip these steps if the companion module already supplies the required places.

1. Press E on the realm to open Map editor.
2. Select Places.
3. Click Add place.
4. Click the starting location and enter its details.
5. Add a second place for the destination.
6. If travelling by road, select Roads and click New road or New trail.
7. Click the start, intermediate bends and destination. Click close enough to place markers to snap the road to them.
8. Press Enter to finish the road.
9. Click Done editing.

Across-country travel does not require a drawn road. Wagons and coaches need a usable road or trail route. Printed roads in the background image do not count as drawn roads.

See [Places and Areas](09-places.md) and [Roads and waterways](09-roads-water.md) for the full editor instructions.

## Set the rules

In Configure Settings > KG Realms, set Travel rules to D&D 2014 or D&D 2024. The Travel panel's Rules tab also lets the GM choose the rule set.

Before continuing, check:

- The correct realm scene is open.
- The group has its intended members.
- The group token is placed at the starting location.
- Players own their character actors.
- The destination exists, or you will choose a point on the map.
- The scale is correct.
