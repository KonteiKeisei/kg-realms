# Trade: player

Audience: player. Required: a visible place with trade information.

1. Open the place's full card.
2. Select Trade.
3. Read the listed price and unit.
4. Open a good's price details for its availability, supplier and description.


The GM can hide goods. The Trade tab is a reference; use the table's normal buying and selling process to exchange money and items.
