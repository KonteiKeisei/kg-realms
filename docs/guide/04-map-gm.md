# Map controls: GM

Audience: GM. Required: [realm and party setup](03-party.md).


## Find a place

1. Press Ctrl+F while viewing a KG Realms map, or click its Search control.
2. Type part of a place name.
3. Choose a result to locate the place.

Linked Regional places are also searchable from the realm. Some Regional-only destinations may have no permanent realm marker.

## Use filters

Toggle Places, Areas and Geography separately. More than one can be active. The combination is remembered in this browser.

Areas contains Domains and Territories. It is independent of the Regional map setup tool.

## Open a place card

1. Hold the pointer over a visible marker or label for its preview.
2. Double-click it for the full card.
3. Click Edit to change the place.

The hover delay is a client setting. A card may include known information, trade details, artwork and a scene link.

## View or Enter a scene

For a linked scene:

- View opens it for you.
- Enter activates it for everyone.

These scene controls are GM-only. Viewing a scene is not the same action as activating it.

## Private information

The GM can inspect hidden and rumoured places. Ordinary place visibility and secret text determine what players can read.

Encounter dots and their preview/full cards are GM-only, including on Regional maps. Their cards use an orange-yellow outline and a GM only badge. See [Encounters](08-encounters-gm.md).

## Move panels

Unlock a panel before dragging its header. Use its lock to keep it in place and its minimise button to fold it. Panels with a resize grip can have their height adjusted.

For map pan and zoom, use the Foundry controls configured on your client. Drawing modes change what map clicks do. Finish or cancel the active editor operation before navigating normally.
