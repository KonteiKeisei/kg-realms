# Encounters: GM

Audience: GM; journey progression is controlled by the Primary GM.

## Choose an encounter type

- Map encounter: placed through Map editor > Encounters. Journeys passing within its reach can meet it.
- Journey encounter: added through Travel > Add encounters to that route.
- River hazard: attached to a drawn river and encountered by journeys following that river.

## Place a map encounter

1. Press E and select Encounters.
2. Click New encounter.
3. Click its location on the map. A nearby road can provide a snap point.
4. Set Name, Perception DC, Range and Group as needed.
5. Open its card to add description, GM notes, images, actors or a scene.
6. Finish editing.

Range is measured in miles from the route. An encounter outside that reach is not met just because it is visible to the GM.


Encounter dots and their preview/full cards are blocked for players regardless of discovery state. Revealing an ordinary place is a separate function.

## Add a journey encounter

1. Plan a route in Travel.
2. Click Add encounters.
3. Click the route to add an encounter.
4. Drag it to reposition it, or open its settings to edit the details.
5. Click Finish adding.

The Encounters tab lists the route's encounters. An off-route warning identifies encounters that will not be met.

## Run and conclude

1. When travel stops at an encounter, review its GM details.
2. Resolve any requested Perception checks.
3. Open the card, artwork, actors or linked scene as needed.
4. Run the encounter.
5. Click Conclude Encounter when finished.
6. Return to the map or continue the camp sequence.

Back to the map activates the map for everyone. Its View control changes only your view. Concluding an encounter and switching scenes are separate actions.

## Multiple parties

If one party blocks another at an encounter, the GM can run it, leave it waiting, or bypass it with a recorded reason when the prompt allows. Select the intended party before resuming its journey.
