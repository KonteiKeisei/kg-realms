# Map controls: player

Audience: player. Required: the GM has prepared and opened a KG Realms map.


## Find and read a place

1. Press Ctrl+F or click Search.
2. Type a place name and choose a result.
3. Hold the pointer over its visible label or marker for a preview.
4. Double-click for the full card.

You can read information the GM has made available. A rumoured place can have less information than a discovered place.

## Choose map categories

Places, Areas and Geography can be enabled together. Changing your filters does not reveal hidden places.

## Adjust your display

Open Configure Settings > KG Realms for client options such as Map marker size, Map label size, Place card hover delay and Window theme.

These options affect your client. The GM sets the world's journey, discovery and rule options.

## Scene changes

The GM controls entering linked scenes and activating maps for the group. A card available to you does not grant GM scene controls.
