# Settlement and local maps: GM

Audience: GM. Required: a realm place and the scene that maps it.

## Link a map

1. Create or import the settlement/site scene.
2. Right-click it in the Scenes sidebar and choose Make local map.
3. Choose the matching Place from the realm.
4. Click Make local map.
5. Review Local map settings.
6. Open the scene.

Existing journal pins can be converted into local places. Review their names, categories, content and visibility after conversion.

If the scene is already linked on a place card, conversion finds that link and prefills the place. Places inherited by a Regional map are included. Hidden places remain available to the GM for setup.

A successful conversion links the place and scene. Check the place card for Enter followed by the place name, and the scene's right-click menu for Local map settings. Existing map tiles remain on the scene.

Companion modules may supply local-map presets. Check the linked place card before creating a duplicate scene.

## Export and scene creation

Export as module includes linked scenes, tiles, walls, lights, local places and referenced artwork. The exported module offers a map checklist on first load and through the Scenes sidebar's Generate button.

An ungenerated linked map has a Generate action on its place card. This includes converted local scenes and their placed tiles. Older companion presets may use Set up the local map instead.

## Regenerate a packaged scene

1. Open its place card and click Regenerate map, or use Regenerate copy in the map checklist.
2. Leave Include scene tokens unticked for a clean map.
3. Click Generate and link new copy.

The new scene becomes the place's linked scene. The previous scene remains in the Scenes sidebar.

## Rebuild local map tiles

1. Open Edit Scene from Token Controls, or right-click the scene and choose Configure.
2. Enter the new picture in Background Image.
3. Click Regenerate local map tiles from Background Image.
4. Confirm and wait for the build to finish.

The build replaces the background tile selection and optimizes standard and overhead image tiles. Each placed tile keeps its position, rotation, elevation, sort, transparency and occlusion settings. Videos remain native. Pins, roads, walls, lights and grid scale remain in place. Maps with multiple Foundry scene levels cannot rebuild their background yet.


## Place entrances

1. Accept Place gate or road in when prompted, then click where the party should arrive overland.
2. Name the entrance on its card.
3. Add other entrances through the Local places editor as needed.
4. For boat arrivals, place a dock entrance when prompted.
5. Mark additional suitable harbour places as A way in to offer other dock arrivals.

The GM chooses a suitable land entrance on arrival. Boat arrivals require a dock. A decorative gate or harbour image alone does not create an entrance.

## Add local places

1. Open Local places from the scene controls.
2. On the Places tab, click Place, then click its position. Gate and Dock start entrance placement instead.
3. Open its card and set its name, category, visibility, information and scene link.
4. Drag its marker in the editor to reposition it.
5. Use Place beside an unplaced imported record to put it on the map.

Link a building or site to another scene through its card. A linked interior can itself become a local map, with return navigation to its parent.

## Draw settlement roads

1. Open Local places > Roads, or the Settlement roads control.
2. Choose Avenue, Street, Lane or Alley.
3. Click Draw road.
4. Click its points and connect them to the existing network.
5. Click Finish road or press Enter.
6. Name the selected road and check its category.

Carriages use avenues and streets. Walking uses every category and avoids alleys where practical. Crossing roads and snapped endpoints connect.

Settlement trip distances use the scene's grid scale. Set the correct grid distance and units before drawing routes or testing timings.

## Plan a local trip

1. Open Plan a trip in this settlement.
2. Choose a destination on the map or search by name.
3. Compare On foot and By coach where a coach route is available.
4. Review Who goes and the fare.
5. Use Edit route to add route points if required.
6. Click Set off.


Each carriage boarding has its own fare. The total accounts for the selected riders and number of rides. Walking connections can form part of a coach trip. The settlement coach workflow handles payment; check character funds if departure is refused.

Use the active trip controls to stop a ride. Unticked members are not passengers and do not pay the fare. Merge the parties later through the party tools.

## Return and style

Use the map navigation trail to return through linked scenes. GM-only navigation distinguishes viewing a parent from activating it for everyone.

Local map settings controls marker scale and icon-pack overrides. Seasonal images require matching content supplied by the map/preset and the relevant seasonal settings.
