# Your first journey

Audience: player. Required: the GM has prepared the party and planned a journey.

1. Click Travel in the realm scene controls.
2. Read the current journey status.
3. Use Route or Rules when those tabs are available.
4. Respond to any roll requested for a character you own.


## What the status means

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| On the road / Sailing | The party moves as the GM advances time |
| Paused | Travel is halted |
| End of the day | The GM must make the next travel or camp decision |
| Camped | The party is in its camp/rest sequence |
| Stopped | Travel is waiting; the GM handles the cause |
| Arrived | The journey has reached its destination |

The GM chooses how much journey information you can see. A missing route or itinerary can be intentional.

## Your controls

- Read the shared journey and rules.
- Choose camp tasks for characters you own when enabled.
- Help arrange watches when the GM allows it.
- Answer requested rolls and rest requests.

The GM controls route changes, departure, time advancement, pauses and scene activation.
