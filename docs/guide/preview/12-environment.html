Biomes, weather, snow and effects

Audience: GM. Required: a realm or Regional map. Calendar and effect integrations require their respective modules.

Calendaria is recommended for travel timekeeping, watches, calendars and seasons. FXMaster and FXMaster+ are compatible/integrated optionals.

Draw a biome zone

  1. Open Map editor > Biomes.
  2. Set New zone's type.
  3. Click Draw manually.
  4. Click the zone's corners.
  5. Press Enter, double-click or choose Finish zone to close it.
  6. Select the zone to edit its name, type and outline.

Unzoned land uses the map's terrain data. A custom image does not acquire terrain categories by recognising its colours.

Trace an existing biome

  1. Choose the target type.
  2. Click Trace map biome.
  3. Click the connected terrain area.
  4. Review the proposed outline and adjust its corners.
  5. Click Save traced zone.

Tracing needs a usable biome grid. If it is unavailable, draw manually.

Day and night

Configure Settings > KG Realms > Day and night selects clock-based, fixed-day or fixed-night appearance. Day starts at and Night starts at set the transition hours.

Clock-based appearance follows world time. With Calendaria installed, its clock participates in that time/calendar workflow. A day/night appearance override does not rewrite the current date.

Realm snow

  1. Enable Seasonal snow in module settings.
  2. Open the realm's Map editor > Snow.
  3. Enable the relevant pole or poles.
  4. Set winter reach, summer reach and patchy edge.
  5. Choose the deepest winter month.
  6. Use See the snow in to preview another month.

Previewing a month does not change the game date. Back to the world's own restores the world's snow defaults. Water boundaries matter for keeping snow on land.

Regional snow

Open Snow while viewing the Regional scene. Check Enable snow line on this regional map only if wanted. It is independent of the realm snow toggle and starts disabled.

Weather and local maps

Biome climates integrate with Calendaria. Linked settlement scenes can use the climate at their realm place. Available seasonal pictures depend on the local-map content; the module does not generate winter artwork from a normal picture.

Effects and performance

FXMaster and FXMaster+ provide supported scene effects. Water regions (FXMaster+) is a world setting. Disable it if its effects cause slow loading or lag.

Names above effects keeps labels, markers and journeys above the scene effects. Turning it off draws them with the map and can reduce display cost. Sharp map on high-DPI screens also increases rendering demand.
