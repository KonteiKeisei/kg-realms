Places, cards and Areas

Audience: GM. Required: an open realm or Regional map.

Open Map editor with E or the scene control. Select Places. The top editing bar describes the current click action. Click Done editing to leave.

Add a place

  1. Click Add place.
  2. Choose its kind: Settlement, Site, Domain, Territory or Natural feature.
  3. Click the map at its location.
  4. Complete the place details and save.
  5. Double-click the marker for its full card and click Edit for additional fields.

Move and style a marker

  1. Select the place in Places.
  2. Drag its marker to move it.
  3. Use the selected place's marker controls to change its symbol.
  4. Use Add marker to supply a custom marker image and name.

On a linked Regional map, moving an inherited place changes its detailed position, not the realm marker's position.

Edit its card

  1. Open the place's full card and click Edit.
  2. Change its name, artwork, known information and other available fields.
  3. Set its visibility for players.
  4. Keep secret text in the provided secret/GM fields.
  5. Choose a scene if the place should open a scene.
  6. Click Save. Cancel or Discard leaves unsaved card edits out of the saved record.

Use Insert place link in the text editor to link another place's card. Select a result from the picker rather than copying a scene name into the text.

Visibility

  • Hidden: unavailable as an ordinary player place.
  • Rumoured: limited place information.
  • Discovered: the place's available player information.

Check the player view after revealing a place. Player visibility does not grant editing or scene activation controls. Encounter dots/cards have their own GM-only restriction.

Draw an Area border

Areas groups Domains and Territories. The border can have any shape.

  1. Select Places > Draw area border.
  2. Choose the existing Domain or Territory, or create one in the prompt.
  3. Click the map to add corners.
  4. Click the first corner, press Enter or choose Finish closed border to close it.
  5. Select the place to adjust its corners, or choose Redraw border to replace the shape.

The Area border is independent of a Regional map's rectangular coverage. Drawing or changing one does not resize the other.

Lists, removal and files

  • Search expands matching place groups. Clear the search to collapse them again.
  • Remove from the map removes a supplied place from this world's map, search and journeys. Use the removed-place list to restore it.
  • Delete place removes a place you added.
  • Reset position restores a supplied place's position. Reset all edits has a wider effect; review before using it.
  • Export places saves the world's place edits and custom markers.
  • Import places replaces the world's place edits after confirmation. Export the current edits first if you need a copy.
