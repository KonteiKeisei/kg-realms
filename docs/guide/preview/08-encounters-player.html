Encounters: player

Audience: player. Required: your party is travelling or camping.

When travel stops

  1. Read any message or roll request addressed to your character.
  2. Roll through the request's control when asked.
  3. Follow the GM's scene change if the encounter uses another scene.
  4. Wait for the GM to conclude the encounter.

The Travel panel does not give players the GM's encounter name or private encounter card. Information shared in chat, artwork or the scene is handled separately.

During camp, an encounter can interrupt the watch sequence. The GM resumes the remaining camp phases after it ends.
